Summary

The final day of the summit had no video program. Instead, the launch of the new hackathon "Pi Next" was announced on Pi's official pages: a prize pool of 200,000 USDT, with final submissions due January 5, 2027 (January 6, 16:59 JST). Up to 20 million Pi in marketing support is a separate program, not part of the prize pool.

  • Pi Next is a hackathon (a short contest in which developers build apps) where developers and creators build new apps that use Pi. It started on November 19, 2026, with a total prize pool of 200,000 USDT (a digital currency tied to the US dollar).
  • The prize pool splits into a Judges' Pick award of 50,000 USDT in total (1st place 25,000, 2nd place 15,000, 3rd place 10,000 USDT) and Community Engagement Awards of 150,000 USDT in total, for up to two teams, decided by how widely the app is used.
  • Up to 20 million Pi in marketing support (the App Growth Initiative, a program that helps apps grow) is a separate program from the prize pool. Under it, Pioneers who use or invite others to use a participating app can receive Pi rewards.
  • There are two ways to build. A Pi app is built in the Developer Web Console (a website where developers manage their Pi apps), registering under the Hackathon tab. A SoloHost app is built and published through SoloHost in Pi Desktop (a way to publish apps from your own computer), with the SoloHost App ID entered at submission. Every team member must register, or the team cannot take part or win prizes.
  • The theme is open. Apps must follow the Mainnet listing requirements, deliver real utility, and include Pi Authentication (Pi sign-in) and Pi Payments. Each entry may submit up to three apps.
  • Final submissions are due Tuesday, January 5, 2027, 23:59 PST (Wednesday, January 6, 2027, 16:59 JST), with one team member submitting per team. The submission must include a demo video of 3 minutes or less (explaining what the app does, who it is for, and how Pi fits in) and the team member list. A midpoint submission is optional; selected apps gain extra visibility and feedback before the final deadline.
  • Day 3 also contained no new announcement on migration or KYC.

Result 12: Pi Next (the Pi Next hackathon kickoff)

Pi Next is a hackathon where developers and creators build new apps that use Pi. It started on November 19, 2026. Developers build their app on Pi's Developer Web Console or on SoloHost and launch it to Pi's ecosystem of more than 60 million engaged users, competing for 200,000 USDT in prizes. Developers, teams, businesses, and startups can enter.

Prizes and support

The total prize pool is 200,000 USDT, split into two awards. Do not confuse them with the marketing support of up to 20 million Pi, which is a separate program.

The Judges' Pick criteria are: alignment with the Mainnet Listing Guidelines; Pi utility and alignment with overall community and ecosystem needs; technical completeness and innovation; UI/UX (how easy the screens are to use), accessibility, and ease of navigation; long-term utility on the Pi Mainnet blockchain; and uniqueness and distinctive use cases. SoloHost apps must meet the same standards of quality, functionality, and utility as Pi apps, but are subject to different listing requirements on SoloHost.

How to enter (two ways to build)

  • Pi app: Create a Pi account and register for Pi Next in the Developer Web Console (a website where developers manage their Pi apps) under the Hackathon tab. All team members must register in order to participate in the hackathon and be considered for prizes. Then follow the console's instructions to build the app.
  • SoloHost app: Create a Pi account and register for Pi Next in the Developer Web Console under the Hackathon tab (again, all team members must register). Download and install Pi Desktop on your computer, then build the app and publish it through SoloHost. Use the AI skill in the public SoloHost GitHub repository — a set of instructions that teaches an AI assistant to do the preparation work — to let an AI coding assistant help prepare the app for publication. Enter your SoloHost App ID where prompted at submission.

What to build, and what to submit

The theme is open-ended: the app can focus on any use case addressing real human needs, as long as it follows the Mainnet Listing Requirements and brings real utility to the Pi Ecosystem. Build an app that people will use, and integrate Pi's built-in features such as Pi Authentication (Pi sign-in) and Pi Payments. Judges will look at what the app offers users and whether they come back. Up to three apps may be submitted per entry, according to the registration page.

Submit the app through the Developer Web Console, including a demo video (3 minutes or less, posted publicly on YouTube or another video platform) and the team member list. The video should cover what the app is and what it does, a walkthrough of its features and interface, who the app is for, the problem or need it addresses, and how Pi is part of the experience, such as Pi sign-in or Pi payments. Every app must also have an English name, a logo that follows Pi's trademark guidelines, an accurate English description, preview images that show the app's interface, integration with PiNet, and a privacy policy. SoloHost apps must follow relevant SoloHost publishing requirements.

Schedule

  • Starts: November 19, 2026 (according to the registration page).
  • Midpoint submission (optional): Submission details will be communicated via email to all participants who register for Pi Next in the Developer Web Console. Selected apps may gain additional visibility and receive valuable feedback to help improve their applications before the final submission deadline.
  • Final submission: Tuesday, January 5, 2027, 11:59 PM PST (Wednesday, January 6, 2027, 07:59 AM UTC; 16:59 JST). Only one team member should submit each app.

Pi Next continues the work announced on Day 1: the Developer Web Console and the MCP server (Model Context Protocol, a standard way for AI programs to connect to outside tools). The official page explains that the console supports AI coding agents, and that the SoloHost AI skill demonstrated on Day 2 can help prepare an app for publication. With Pi Launchpad, DEX, and AMM coming to Mainnet in Q1 2027, Pi Next participants also get time to prepare for token issuance (as announced on Day 1).
